Watching water boil, one dot at a time.

Heat a fixed mass of H₂O at constant pressure, held there by a weighted piston. The vessel on the left shows the liquid and vapor in it; the dot on the right traces the same state on the T–v diagram — through compressed liquid (l), saturated liquid (f), the two-phase mixture, saturated vapor (g), and superheated vapor (s).

Try it: pick a pressure below, then drag the heat slider or press Play. The slider's width per phase is scaled by how much heat that phase actually takes — watch how much of it goes to boiling alone.
